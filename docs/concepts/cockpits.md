## Purpose
Created: 2026-10-11
Modified: 2026-10-11

A cockpit is a center that orients Lion: what he was doing, what his next steps are, where he is going, and the contextual information relevant to that activity. Different cockpits need different things. Their purpose includes helping compensate for ADHD-related distractibility.

## Four major loci of interaction
Created: 2026-10-11
Modified: 2026-10-11

- [Today](today.md): daily context and records, with navigation between days.
- [Projects](projects.md): project context and tracking across multiple days.
- [Focus](focus.md): attention management at a given time.
- [Arcs](arcs.md): passages of life and their causal and other relationships.

These are related areas with overlapping concerns. Their precise integration and division of interface responsibilities remain to be designed. A common panel system is described for Today and planned for Projects; a shared layout for all four has not been established.

Lion identifies [FileTalk](filetalk.md) as the mechanism through which Focus, Arcs, Today, and the Project Browser to come will communicate with each other.
