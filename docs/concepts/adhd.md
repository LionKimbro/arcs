## Personal design context
Created: 2026-10-11
Modified: 2026-10-11

Lion reports having very strong ADHD. Part of the purpose of his cockpits and related machinery is to compensate for his distractibility, helping him situate himself, recover context, and maintain continuity. This is a user-stated design requirement; it does not by itself specify particular interface features or constitute a clinical assessment.
